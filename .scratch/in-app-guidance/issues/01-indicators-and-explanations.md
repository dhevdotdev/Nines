# In-app guidance for what stats/indicators mean

Status: needs-triage

The app surfaces a growing set of derived numbers and indicators (streaks, momentum, gaps, target-vs-actual, per-day dot states in the history strip) with no built-in explanation of what they mean or how to read them. As the app grows, unexplained derived metrics get harder to interpret, not easier — this doesn't need a big help/onboarding system, just something lightweight per metric.

No design decided yet: where the explanation lives (tap-to-reveal, a small info affordance next to each stat, a one-time onboarding pass), how much detail is appropriate (a tooltip vs. a real explanation of the EWMA/decay model), and whether this should be consistent everywhere or tailored per screen.
